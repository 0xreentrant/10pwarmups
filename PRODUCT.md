# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BJJ practitioners training 10th Planet Jiu-Jitsu warmup sequences (partnered drill flows), typically pre-class at the gym on a phone, sometimes reviewing at home on desktop. The job: memorize a fixed sequence of moves for a partnered warmup drill well enough to run it from memory, using quiz-style recall ("what's next?") rather than passive video watching.

## Product Purpose

10th Planet Warmup Trainer (openthesystem.app) turns a library of BJJ warmup drill videos (originally posted on Instagram) into a spaced-recall training tool: pick a deck, get quizzed move-by-move, build a streak, track progress across decks/series over time. Success = a user can execute a full warmup sequence from memory without watching the video.

## Brand Personality

Gritty, no-frills, gym-tough. Dark, utilitarian, mono-typewriter body copy, condensed uppercase display type, flame/streak/heat motifs for progress. No polish-for-polish's-sake - reads like a tool built by someone who trains, not a marketing site.

## Anti-references

Not a SaaS dashboard. Avoid rounded cards, soft shadows, gradient hero sections, generic light-mode "wellness app" styling. Avoid anything that feels like it's selling a subscription rather than serving a training session.

## Design Principles

- Recall over playback - the core loop is testing memory, not watching video; any video feature supports that loop, it doesn't replace it.
- One-handed, gym-usable - large tap targets, minimal chrome, works with sweaty hands on a phone between rounds.
- Show state at a glance - streaks, heat bars, partner-color-coding (green/blue) carry information without extra reading.
- Stay in the flow - popovers/confirms over full navigations; don't break the training session to show supplementary content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No stated WCAG target; keep default text contrast (light text on near-black) and support reduced motion for flame/streak animations, consistent with existing patterns in the codebase.
