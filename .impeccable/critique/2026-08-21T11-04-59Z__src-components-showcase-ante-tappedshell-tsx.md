## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM**: Not AI slop. Gym stamp, not SaaS fail screen. Domain voice + condensed display + mono body on-brand. Soft risk: cream CTA glow if overused elsewhere.

**Deterministic**: CLI 0 on TappedShell/AnteBits. Browser: tp-note all-caps-body; tp-action dark-glow + thin-border-wide-shadow; tp-caption low-contrast 3.3:1. Showcase chrome FPs for blurb/body stripes.

## Overall Impression

Slap In is the stamp energy north star. Messaging + simple meta work because hierarchy is the craft: one word-mark, one human cost line, one quiet strip, one glowing slap. Soften empty meta and floor the CTA and peak-end lands harder without adding chrome.

## What's Working

1. Cost copy like a training partner - teaches miss without UI lecture.
2. Meta as quiet strip not dashboard - gym-tough restraint.
3. One saturated object - cream CTA vs near-black veil.
